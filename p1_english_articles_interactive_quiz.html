Primary 1 English Revision Quiz

Focus Topic: Articles (a / an / the / ✕)

Part A: Sentences Completion 共 14 分
選擇最合適的冠詞填入空格：a / an / the，若不需要冠詞請選 ✕。
1. Peter is student. He eats orange every morning.
2. Look at sun in the sky! It is very bright today.
3. Mum bought me nice schoolbag. schoolbag is purple.
4. I have ham sandwich and glass of milk for breakfast.
5. Miss Chan is kind teacher. She teaches us English.
6. They like to play with their classmates at recess.
7. There is owl sitting on oak tree outside the window.
8. My father goes to work by bus. He has egg tart for his snack.
Part B: Story Passage Completion 共 10 分
閱讀短文，選擇合適的冠詞（a / an / the）完成故事。
One Sunday morning, Mary went to (1) park with her parents. While walking along the path, they saw (2) cute yellow bird and (3) little squirrel near (4) tall oak tree.

(5) yellow bird was building (6) nest high up in the branches. Two days later, they went back to (7) oak tree to see the nest. It was not (8) empty nest! There was (9) tiny egg inside. Mary felt very happy because the yellow bird would have (10) baby bird soon.

測驗成績報告

總分：0 / 24 分

Articles 核心規則解析：

  • a / an (泛指單數可數名詞)： 母音音素開頭（a, e, i, o, u）用 an（如 an orange, an owl, an egg tart, an empty nest）；子音音素開頭用 a（如 a student, a glass）。
  • the (特定或獨一無二事物)： 全球獨一無二自然天體（如 the sun）；文章中再次提及同一特定事物時改用 the（如 the schoolbag, the yellow bird, the oak tree）。
  • ✕ (不加冠詞)： 語言學科名稱前（如 teaches us ✕ English）；交通方式片語（如 by ✕ bus）；名詞前已有形容詞性物主代名詞（如 their ✕ classmates）。
