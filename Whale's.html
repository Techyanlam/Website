關於我們

Whale's Capital Company Limited 是專業商務轉介服務供應商，為合作夥伴配對優質商業機會。


我們搜集並篩選各行各業的合適商機，涵蓋飛機買賣、藝術品與收藏品、保險業務、AI項目、物業、供應鏈貿易、酒店旅遊、可再生能源、醫療健康及特許經營。

透過多個渠道收集潛在項目，完成初步資料核實，過濾低質素線索。我們的核心目標，是將經驗證、可行的商機交到夥伴手上，讓你毋須耗費大量前期功夫尋找項目。

About Us

Whale's Capital Company Limited is a specialist referral service provider, connecting quality deal opportunities to our business partners.


We source and screen qualified business opportunities across multiple sectors, including aircraft trading, fine art & collectibles, insurance, AI projects, real estate, supply chain, hospitality, renewable energy, healthcare and franchise business.

We gather potential deals through multiple channels, complete initial verification and filter out low-quality leads. Our core mission is to hand over verified, viable opportunities for our partners to grow and expand their business without heavy upfront legwork.

創辦理念

Whale's Capital 的成立，源於創辦人王賢訊先生多年來於金融、商業及公共服務領域累積的深厚經驗與廣闊人脈。王先生深信，市場上從不缺乏商機，缺乏的是一座可信、高效的橋樑——將經核實的優質項目，準確交到合適的合作夥伴手上。


本公司因此應運而生：以嚴謹專業的篩選流程、跨行業的人脈網絡，為合作夥伴帶來真正有價值、可落地的商業機會，讓每位夥伴都能專注於洽談與促成交易，而毋須為搜尋商機耗費大量時間與資源。

Our Founding Concept

Whale's Capital was founded on the deep experience and extensive network that our founder, Mr. Vincent Wong, has built across finance, business and public service over the years. He believes the market never lacks opportunities — what it lacks is a trusted, efficient bridge that connects verified, high-quality projects with the right partners.


This company was created for exactly that purpose: a rigorous, professional screening process combined with a cross-industry network, delivering genuinely valuable and actionable opportunities to our partners — so they can focus on negotiation and deal closing, without spending enormous time and resources hunting for leads.

展望

展望未來，Whale's Capital 將持續擴大轉介夥伴網絡，深化與大灣區及國際市場的連結，並積極探索人工智能於商機配對上的應用，進一步提升核實效率與配對精準度。


我們期望成為亞太區最值得信賴的商務轉介平台，與每一位合作夥伴攜手，開拓更廣闊的商業版圖，共創長遠價值。

Outlook

Looking ahead, Whale's Capital will continue to expand its referral partner network, deepen its connections with the Greater Bay Area and international markets, and actively explore the application of artificial intelligence in deal matching — further enhancing verification efficiency and matching accuracy.


We aspire to become the most trusted business referral platform in the Asia-Pacific region, working hand in hand with every partner to unlock broader horizons and create lasting value together.
