Section B: Fill in each blank with: a / an / the / X (15 marks)
Example: I am a student. (Choose X if no article is needed)
My Pet Cat
I have (1) pet cat. Her name is Mimi. She is (2) orange and white cat.
Mimi has (3) small nose and (4) long tail. She has (5) soft fur.
Every morning, Mimi drinks (6) glass of milk. She eats (7) fish for lunch.
In the afternoon, I play with (8) cat in (9) garden. (10) cat likes to chase butterflies.
Mimi sleeps on (11) big cushion. She is (12) good pet. I love (13) cat very much.
At night, Mimi eats (14) egg as a treat. She has (15) happy life with our family.

得分：0 / 25 分

Articles 核心規則解析：

  • Section A (a vs an): 母音音素開頭（a, e, i, o, u）用 an（如 an umbrella, an onion, an ant, an apple, an egg）；子音音素開頭用 a（如 a horse, a jacket, a tomato, a bee, a pencil）。
  • Section B (Articles in Context):
    • X (不加冠詞)： 不可數名詞（soft fur）與食物肉類名詞（eats fish for lunch）前不加冠詞。
    • the (特定人事物)： 文章中再次提及已出現過的同一隻貓時，特指該事物須用 the cat（第 8、10、13 題）以及特定地點 the garden（第 9 題）。
    • a / an (泛指初次提及)： 初次提及單數寵物、特徵（a small nose, a long tail）及單位量詞（a glass of milk）。
